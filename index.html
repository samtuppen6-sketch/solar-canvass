Why it closes deals
  • Instant qualification. Green tick or red cross, live from GOV.UK — no guesswork, no wasted pitches on properties that were never eligible.
  • A visual the customer instantly gets. They see their own home glowing orange near the bottom of the scale, with green A right at the top. It lands harder than any letter on a form.
  • The "what it could be" hook. The register's own potential rating shows the customer exactly how far their home could climb after upgrades — straight from an official source.
  • Built-in accuracy guardrail. If a property comes back as A–C, the app blocks the sign-up before a deal is logged. No more non-qualifying deals slipping through.
"You're sat in the orange here — but your own energy certificate says this house could be a C. That's the difference these upgrades make."

Compliance

Data-driven targeting, used responsibly.

Solpath provides territory intelligence as an initial guide only. Eligibility is subject to survey, assessment, scheme rules and installer checks. Final determination rests with the installer and scheme administrator.

Data sources

Public postcode eligibility data, GOV.UK EPC ratings and area indicators — used as initial guidance only.

Privacy & GDPR

No resident personal data stored or sold. Every organisation's data — doors knocked, outcomes, deals — is fully isolated and visible only to that organisation's team. One firm's records are never shared with or visible to another.

Do-not-knock

Suppression process for flagged properties. Clients comply with local canvassing regulations.

Legitimate interest

Canvassing remains subject to the client's own legitimate interest assessment and obligations.

Client responsibility

Clients ensure teams operate within applicable laws and scheme rules. Solpath does not direct activity.

Scheme eligibility

Indicators are a canvassing tool, not a determination. Final decisions rest with installer and administrator.
