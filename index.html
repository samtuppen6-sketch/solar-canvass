The problem

Your team should not be planning
territories from spreadsheets.

Spreadsheets go stale

Static lists cannot reflect live scheme eligibility, changing EPC data, or recent visit outcomes. Reps work with information that was already out of date when they printed it.

Google Maps does not know scheme logic

Generic navigation tools have no concept of EPC ratings, LSOA deprivation bands, or scheme eligibility. Reps are navigating blind to whether a street is worth visiting at all.

Reps waste time on unsuitable streets

Without property-level indicators, canvassers spend the same time on clearly ineligible homes as on likely target properties. The ratio of useful doors to wasted doors is simply too low.

Callbacks and outcomes get lost

Interested customers who asked to be called back disappear into note apps, WhatsApp threads, or nothing. No visibility, no follow-up, no conversion.

Solpath gives the team one shared view of where to go, what to check, and what happened at the door — updated in real time, on the map, before a rep sets foot on a street.

How it works

Up and running the same day.

No data exports, no IT project. Your team can be working the map within hours.

01

Select your territory

Choose a region, district, postcode patch, or campaign area. Solpath loads the relevant property and eligibility data for that territory.

02

Surface likely target properties

Solpath uses available postcode, property, EPC and area indicators to highlight likely opportunities — before reps arrive on the street.

03

Reps work the map

Canvassers navigate by street on their phone, tap each property, and log outcomes in seconds — qualified, callback, no answer, not suitable.

04

Outcomes flow back to your team

Callbacks, interested customers and captured actions are available for follow-up, manager review, CRM export, or your existing survey flow.

Platform features

Everything your canvassing
operation needs.

Territory intelligence

Postcode and area data layered with scheme eligibility indicators, surfaced on a live map before reps arrive.

Property-level indicators

EPC band, property type, and tenure data used to rank and filter likely opportunity addresses within a territory.

Rep map workflow

Mobile-first canvassing interface with street-level navigation, property tap-to-select, and one-tap outcome logging.

Flag properties for follow-up with timestamped notes. All callbacks aggregated in a single tab for the team.

Manager dashboard

Multi-region setup

Deploy separate regions, campaigns, or territories for different teams, clients, or scheme types from a single account.

Compliance

Data-driven targeting,
used responsibly.

Solpath provides territory intelligence as an initial guide only. Eligibility is subject to survey, assessment, scheme rules and installer checks. Final determination rests with the installer and scheme administrator. All property indicators and EPC data shown are indicative only — they do not constitute a guarantee or assessment of eligibility.

Data sources

Property targeting uses publicly available postcode eligibility data, GOV.UK EPC register ratings, and area-level deprivation indicators. Used as initial targeting guidance only.

Privacy & GDPR

Solpath does not store or sell personal data about residents. Door outcome records are held per installer account and are subject to our Privacy Policy and applicable UK data protection law.

Do-not-knock / suppression

We maintain a suppression process for flagged properties. Clients are responsible for complying with applicable canvassing regulations and local authority requirements in their area.

Legitimate interest

Use of Solpath for canvassing is subject to the client's own legitimate interest assessment and compliance obligations. Solpath provides territory intelligence — canvassing compliance remains the client's responsibility.

Client responsibility

Clients must ensure their canvassing teams operate within applicable laws, scheme rules, and local regulations. Solpath does not direct canvassing activity or act as a scheme administrator.

Scheme eligibility

Solpath indicators are a canvassing tool, not an eligibility determination. All final eligibility decisions rest with the installer, surveyor, and the relevant scheme administrator.
