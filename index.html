The problem

Most canvassing teams are still working blind.

Spreadsheets, postcode lists and Google Maps do not tell your reps which homes are worth approaching. Teams lose time knocking ineligible properties, callbacks get missed, managers lack visibility, and good territories are not worked properly.

🚪

Reps waste time on the wrong doors

Without property-level intelligence, canvassers knock at random — spending the same time on definitely ineligible homes as likely eligible ones.

📋

EPC and property data sits in separate systems

EPC ratings, property types and grant eligibility indicators are scattered across different tools — none of them designed for door-to-door use.

👁️

Managers can't see what happened at each property

No live visibility of territory coverage. Managers rely on reps self-reporting at end of day — if at all.

📞

Callbacks and notes get lost

Interested customers who asked to be called back disappear into note apps, scraps of paper, or nothing at all.

📊

Sales teams struggle to prove territory coverage

No record of which streets were worked, which doors were knocked, or what outcomes were recorded where.

The solution

Solpath turns grant canvassing into a mapped workflow.

Unlike generic canvassing apps, Solpath is built specifically around ECO, Warm Homes and retrofit targeting data — giving reps the right properties to visit before they arrive.

01

Eligibility-led map

Show reps likely target homes based on area and property indicators, before they set foot on a street.

02

EPC targeting

Prioritise D, E, F, G and unrated properties — the homes most likely to qualify for energy improvement schemes.

03

Door outcome tracking

Record no answer, callback, qualified, not qualified and not interested — one tap per door, logged in real time.

04

Callback management

Add notes and revisit instructions so no lead is lost. Every callback address is listed in one place.

05

Sales link routing

Send interested customers straight into your existing application form, CRM or survey URL — no re-entry required.

06

Team visibility

Give managers a clearer view of activity, outcomes and territory coverage across the whole team.

How it works

Up and running the same day.

No complex onboarding, no data exports, no IT project. Your team can be using Solpath in the field within hours.

01

Select your territory

Choose a region, district or specific postcode patch. Solpath loads likely target properties based on eligibility data, EPC ratings and property indicators for that area.

02

Canvassers work the map

Reps see a live map of target homes on their phone. They navigate by street, tap each property, and record the door outcome in one tap.

03

Callbacks and notes are captured

Interested customers get flagged for callback with notes. No lead gets lost. Every address is logged with time and outcome.

04

Qualified leads go to your flow

Interested customers are pushed straight into your existing application form, survey or CRM — no double entry, no spreadsheet transfer.

Compliance

Data-driven targeting, used responsibly.

Solpath uses available data to identify likely target properties. It does not guarantee eligibility. Final eligibility is subject to survey, assessment, scheme rules and installer checks. All property indicators and EPC data shown are initial indicators only.

📂 Data sources

Property targeting draws on publicly available postcode and area eligibility data, GOV.UK EPC register ratings, and property-level indicators. Data is used as an initial targeting guide. We do not represent any data as definitive or guaranteed.

🔒 Privacy and GDPR

Solpath does not store or sell personal data about residents. Door outcome records are held per installer account. Usage is subject to our Privacy Policy and applicable UK data protection law. View Privacy Policy →

🚫 Do not knock / suppression

We maintain a suppression process for properties flagged as do-not-knock or on registered preference lists. Clients are responsible for complying with applicable canvassing regulations and local authority requirements in their operating area.

⚖️ Legitimate interest

Use of Solpath for canvassing activities is subject to the client's own legitimate interest assessment and compliance obligations. Solpath provides territory intelligence — the canvassing activity and its compliance remain the client's responsibility.

🏢 Client responsibility

Clients are responsible for ensuring their canvassing teams operate within applicable laws, scheme rules, and local regulations. Solpath does not direct canvassing activity, make eligibility decisions, or act as a scheme administrator.
